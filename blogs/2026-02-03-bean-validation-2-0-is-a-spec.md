It is done — after one year of hard work, and a bit more than four years after the previous revision, the final release of Bean Validation 2.0 (JSR 380) is out! Last week, the JSR passed the Final Approval Ballot by the executive committee of the JCP unanimously with 25 "Yes" votes. After the ballot we’ve released version 2.0.0.Final of the specification, the API as well as the TCK.
