Our proposed final draft is due soon but we did one last drop of the specification and the API jar. We worked all over the board (and the clock) but the most notable improvements are: Improvements on the CDI integration section We made it much more descriptive of the expected behavior instead of imposing an implementation pattern. We have also added the method and constructor interception priority as defined in the Java EE specification and the interceptor specification in particular.
