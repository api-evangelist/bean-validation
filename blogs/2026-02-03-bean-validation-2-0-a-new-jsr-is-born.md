Today I've got some great news to share with you: a new revision of the Bean Validation spec is about to be kicked off! Over the last weeks, we've been busy with preparing a proposal for this JSR and I've submitted it to the JCP (Java Community Process) last week. You can find the proposal for "JSR 380: Bean Validation 2.0" on jcp.org.
